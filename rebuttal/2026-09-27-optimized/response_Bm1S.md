Response to Reviewer Bm1S

**1. What is composed.** CASS composes a corrective operator: the selected skill bases define a projector, and their coefficient-weighted anchors define its reference state. The unseen task's demonstrations supply the task-specific direction, including its component outside the dictionary span. We will state this division of labor explicitly in the abstract and introduction. Reconstruction-only steering tests a stronger hypothesis—synthesizing the entire new direction from existing skills—which the direction ablation does not support.

We isolated the submitted hybrid under exactly the same routing assignments and replacement predictions as z-only. On the three shared seeds, LOTO accuracy is 0.4575 versus 0.4210: +3.65 percentage points, paired task-bootstrap 95% CI [0.65, 7.27]. This comparison retains de-sharing and direction-norm calibration in both arms and measures the adaptive weighting plus projected correction under the submitted policy.

We also investigated the operator bottleneck in a separate development-selected extension. It contrasts clean demonstrations with the same query without demonstrations, and corrects the shifted state toward a composed positive-context anchor during prefill. All settings were selected on 12 development tasks and frozen. On the 20 non-development LOTO targets, Novel15 and Compound10, it obtains 0.5022/0.6555/0.4694, versus 0.4872/0.6290/0.4544 when only its correction is disabled. The paired gains are +1.50 [0.03, 3.03], +2.65 [0.57, 5.29] and +1.50 [0.40, 2.67] points. These are additional experiments with three new demonstration seeds, not replacements for the submitted results. They provide direct evidence for the narrower corrective-composition claim. With 50 additional disjoint queries per task, full/off accuracies are 0.5050/0.4867, 0.6458/0.6209 and 0.4640/0.4587. The Compound prefix gain is +0.53 [-0.27, 1.27] points; under the stricter literal-first-line metric it is +1.40 [0.40, 2.40]. Both metrics are reported.

A separately position-tuned dictionary-free baseline scores 0.4379/0.6487/0.3055, and 0.4757/0.6444/0.3180 on additional queries. This system comparison is distinct from disabling the same operator’s correction.

**2. Maximum coherence and the guarantee.** We recomputed all five submitted full dictionaries and all 160 submitted LOTO dictionaries. Full-dictionary maximum block coherences are 0.9946 (Llama-3.1), 0.9921 (Llama-3.2), 0.9882 (Gemma-2), 0.9937 (Qwen3), and 0.9935 (Qwen2.5). Your interpretation is correct: the original unweighted sufficient condition permits only one active block here. The median cannot substitute for the maximum.

We rederived the result for the actual penalty weights q_t=sqrt(r_t). The corrected proof uses strict off-support KKT inequalities for the restricted solution. Its conservative coherence corollary is mu_B < 1/(sR+s-1), where s is support size and R is the maximum-to-minimum penalty-weight ratio. Equal weights recover 1/(2s-1). The corrected global bound permits only s=1 for Llama-3.2 and both Qwen models, and certifies no nonempty support for Llama-3.1 or Gemma-2.

The sharper support-specific weighted condition is non-vacuous for some two-block sets. Checking all 496 pairs in each full dictionary gives 10/1/1/2/0 pairs with an upper bound below one, in the model order above. For example, Llama-3.1's park-country/word-length pair has an upper bound 0.9418. This does not certify the Llama Compound constituents or the observed signatures' noise and lambda assumptions. We will replace the claimed broad empirical certification with these precise conditional results and complete proofs. A separate direct perturbation bound addresses the implemented hybrid's direction, anchor, projector and gate errors.

**3. Causal role of support.** We performed the requested intervention on the submitted operator. Wrong supports exclude both inferred and true constituents, preserve block ranks and support size, and match mean/maximum within-support coherence within 0.02/0.03. Eighty-four of 90 alternatives pass, with valid controls for every task–seed pair. Keeping the inferred direction, norm calibration and gate fixed, inferred support scores 0.1477 versus 0.1016 for matched wrong supports: +4.61 points [0.58, 9.72]. Under identical submitted routing and replacement outputs, the difference is +4.11 [0.00, 9.39].

For the new operator, inferred, correction-off and full true-support accuracies are 0.4694, 0.4544 and 0.4614. Thus replacing the support labels with the known constituents does not explain the execution improvement. The intervention supports a causal contribution of the selected corrective geometry; it does not establish sequential execution of named task functions. We will make that distinction explicit throughout the compositional interpretation.
